Lumi privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026. Contact: github.com/makiyakinabe/lumi/issues

Lumi is a free app that helps children and young people with special needs through their day: routines, feelings, talking with pictures, calm-down tools and real-life practice. It is made to be used with a parent, carer, teacher or therapist.

In short

  • Lumi does not collect, send or sell any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything you put into Lumi stays on your device.

What Lumi keeps, and where

Lumi keeps the things you add, only on the device you use it on: the child's name and age group, Lumi's look, the timetable and Coming up events, photos you choose, voice recordings you make, talk-board words, feelings and break choices, and progress such as stars and badges. Nobody else can see them, including us. Deleting Lumi from the device deletes all of it.

Backups

Grown-ups can save a backup file and load it again (Grown-ups › Child). The file is saved wherever you choose. Lumi does not upload it anywhere.

Features that use the internet (off unless a grown-up turns them on)

  • Microphone in Chat. If a grown-up turns it on, Lumi uses the device's own speech service to turn speech into words. That service is provided by Apple or Google and may process the sound on their servers under their own privacy policies. Lumi saves nothing.
  • Live bus times. If a grown-up adds a bus stop and a relay link, Lumi asks that relay for bus arrival times (Singapore's LTA DataMall) for that stop. Only the bus stop number is sent.

Turning on these features needs a grown-up check inside the app.

Children

Lumi is designed for children and follows the rules app stores set for children's apps: no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no personal information leaves the device. Grown-up settings are behind a press-and-hold button and a grown-ups-only check.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: only when a grown-up adds a photo (for example of the child's school or toothbrush).
  • Microphone: only when a grown-up records their own voice for Lumi, or turns on talking to Lumi.
  • Notifications: only if a grown-up turns on reminders for the timetable.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
